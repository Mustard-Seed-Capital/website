Support

Email info@mcombizz.com and we’ll answer within 2 business days. Include your phone model and what happened.

I bought coins or VIP on a new phone and they’re missing.

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Remove ads and VIP come back right away. Coins you already spent can’t be restored.

How do I cancel VIP Club?

VIP is billed by Apple. Open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then VIP Club. Cancel at least a day before it renews.

I want a refund.

Apple handles refunds for purchases in the app. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I cash out my coins?

No. Coins are for fun only and have no cash value. They can’t be cashed out, traded or sent to other players.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the app erases it, and it doesn’t move to a new phone in this version.

Do I need the internet to play?

No for games against the AI players. Yes for reward videos and purchases.

Where are the rules?

Rules for every mode are in the game under Rules, including the card order for Joker Joker Deuce Deuce.

Report a player or a problem

Tap any player at the table to mute, block or report them. For anything else, email us. A person reads every message.
