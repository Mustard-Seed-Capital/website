Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Bags & Books (“the app”) is made by Mustard Seed Capital LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What stays on your phone

Your player name, avatar, level, coins, rank badge, game history and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them, and we don’t ask you to create an account.

Purchases

Coin packs, Remove ads and VIP Club are sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment. We never see your name, card number or Apple Account details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase happened, so the app can unlock it.

Ads

The app offers optional reward videos through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address, the ads you see and tap, and crash and performance data. If you allow tracking when Apple’s prompt asks, Google may use this to show personalized ads. If you don’t, Google shows non-personalized ads. Google’s use of this information is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your email, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location or health information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we’ll help remove it.

Your choices

You can turn off tracking any time in your iPhone’s Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Deleting the app deletes everything saved on your phone. Purchases you made can be restored with Restore Purchases in the app’s Settings.

Security and retention

Because your game data stays on your device, it’s protected by your phone’s own security and kept until you delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and post the new version at this address.
