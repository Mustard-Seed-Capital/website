Game modes

  • Classic SpadesPartners, ace of spades high. Play to 100, 300 or 500.
  • Joker Joker Deuce DeuceBig Joker, Little Joker, 2♦ and 2♠ sit on top of the spades.
  • Free-for-AllNo partners. Every book is yours. First to 100 wins.
  • Weekend CupThree rounds against tougher crews. Lose once and you’re out.

Fair for everyone

The card tracker is free for every player. Nobody buys better cards or a better shuffle. Coins are for fun only: they have no cash value and can’t be cashed out, traded or sent to other players.

Level up

Build your own avatar and level it up with every book you take. Earn a rank badge from Bronze to Ace of Spades and defend it, claim a daily bonus, finish daily missions and unlock new tables.
